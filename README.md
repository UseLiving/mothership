# MOTHERSHIP

The galaxy's memecoins, live on deck.

- **Radar:** every new pump.fun launch streams in live as an alien signal; migrations to PumpSwap land in the warp lane.
- **Sim Deck:** paper trade with 100 SOL at live Jupiter prices.
- **Arcade:** Rug Invaders, with bosses, power-ups and live launches as targets.
- **Abduct:** scan any Solana wallet into an alien species.
- **Launch:** sealed pump.fun launches with a capped dev buy.

Everything runs in the browser. Paper trades are simulated; live trades and launches are real, wallet-signed Solana transactions.
